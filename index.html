The problem

You're one lapsed document away from a job site shutdown.

COIs Expire Without Warning

Your sub's insurance lapsed last week. You didn't know until the inspector showed up. Now the job is stopped and the client is calling.

Chasing Paperwork Is a Full-Time Job

Emailing subs for W-9s, calling for updated COIs, checking license databases — it's hours every week that don't build anything.

One Missing Doc = Liability Exposure

If a sub gets hurt on your site and their COI is expired, you're holding the bag. The risk is real, and spreadsheets don't track it.

How it works

Add your subs. Upload docs. SubComply handles the rest.

Add

Track

Alert

Step 1

Add Your Subcontractors

Enter your subs or import from your PM tool. SubComply creates a compliance profile for each one — COI, W-9, license, insurance.

Step 2

Upload or Request Documents

Upload existing docs or send a request link directly to your sub. They upload, SubComply parses expiration dates and coverage automatically.

Step 3

Get Alerted Before Expiration

Automatic reminders at 30, 14, and 7 days. If a sub doesn't respond, they're flagged as non-compliant for all active jobs.
