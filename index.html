The problem

Manual subcontractor tracking in spreadsheets creates compliance gaps and liability exposure.

01

Expired COI = you're liable. A sub's COI expires mid-project. You don't catch it. Work gets done. Insurance doesn't cover the claim. That's on you.

02

Chasing paperwork from 20 subs per project. You spend 2-3 hours per week just emailing subs for missing COIs, W-9s, and license renewals. It never ends.

03

Spreadsheet tracking breaks at scale. Excel files get outdated. Tabs don't sync. Dates get missed. When audits come, you're scrambling to prove compliance.

04

Audits catch gaps you missed. Your insurance carrier or a project owner audits your files. They find missing docs, expired certs, and incomplete records. You look unprofessional.

How it works

Add your subs. SubComply handles the rest.

Step 1

Add your subs

Upload a list or add subs manually. SubComply automatically requests COIs, W-9s, and license docs from each sub via email. No manual follow-ups needed.

Step 2

AI reads & validates docs

SubComply's AI reads COIs, W-9s, and licenses. It flags missing info, gaps in coverage, and expiration dates. Everything is verified and stored securely.

Step 3

Auto-reminders & alerts

SubComply sends automatic reminders to subs 30 days before expiry. You get a dashboard alert when docs are updated. Stay ahead of deadlines, never chase paperwork again.
